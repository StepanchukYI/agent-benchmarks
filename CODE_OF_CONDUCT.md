# Code of Conduct

agent-benchmarks adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent. Clarify before escalating.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Report issues to the maintainer (see `SECURITY.md`).
- Maintainer decisions are final for v1; governance evolves once ≥ 3 active contributors (per pending ADR).

## Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the project at maintainer discretion. Appeals: reply to the enforcement notice within 14 days.
