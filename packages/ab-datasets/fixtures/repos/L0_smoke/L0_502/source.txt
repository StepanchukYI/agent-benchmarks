In a 2024 study of municipal recycling programs, Dr. Lin and colleagues
observed that participation rates rose by 27% in cities that adopted
single-stream collection, compared with a 4% rise in cities that retained
sortable bins. The authors attribute most of the gap to "the reduction
in household-level sorting friction."

The study covered 41 cities across four U.S. regions over an 18-month
window. It did not measure contamination rates in the recovered stream,
nor the downstream cost-per-ton at the materials recovery facilities.
